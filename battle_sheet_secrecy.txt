######################################################################################
Pseudo-code used for secrecy/reveal aspects of multi-client Positioning Step in combat

The "classes" below are abstractions to train the agent SOLELY for the topic of
Secret vs Reveal in multi-player battles where each player has their own client
and battle frames.  The information each player sees in their battle frame cannot
give away any secret information re other player's decisions during the Position Step
of a battle round.

The primary concept below is encompassed in the interface Revealable.  everything
that may or may not be hidden due to its secrecy is a Revealable.  The faux classes
below (they are not true RS classes) define the relationships the agent needs to understand
re battle sheet secrecy, and are followed by a set of secrecy rules that the agent needs
to apply when implementing this idea in RS.

After the agent is trained on these relationships and and the rules, its job is to map
the faux classes below to actual entities in RS and figure out where RS may need changes
in order for RS to follow the BSS rules as listed at the end of this file.

The agent only proposes changes where the RS code does not already work correctly wrt
secrecy.  And in future I recommend that this file be saved in the repo and amended as
and re-applied in the same manner.  It is a complex thing to keep straight without the
concept and rules being spelled out in one place (here).

Rules follow after pseudo-code.
######################################################################################

interface Revealable:
    abstract is_revealed()

interface HorseRider implements Revealable:
    abstract is_revealed()

Character:
    _owner - Player which owns the character
    ...

abstract Denizen implements Revealable:
    _owner - a Player object.  May be None.
    _character - a Character object.  May be None.
    ...

abstract ControlledDenizen extends Denizen:
    _owner - Player which owns _character.
    _character - Character which controls the ControlledDenizen.

Hireling extends ControlledDenizen implements HorseRider:
    _horse - NativeHorse if the Hireling has one.  May be None.
    is_revealed() -> bool
    ...

ControlledMonster extends ControlledDenizen:
    _weapon - MonsterWeapon if the ControlledMonster has one.  May be None.
    is_revealed() -> bool
    ...

UncontrolledDenizen extends Denizen implements HorseRider:
    _owner = None
    _character = None
    _horse - NativeHorse if the UncontrolledDenizen has one.  May be None.
    _weapon - MonsterWeapon if the UncontrolledDenizen has one.  May be None.
    is_revealed() -> bool
    ...

NativeHorse implements Revealable:
    _owner - Player which owns _rider.  May be None.
    _rider - HorseRider native (Hireling or UncontrolledDenizen) who is riding this NativeHorse
    is_revealed() = _rider.is_revealed()
    ...

MonsterWeapon implements Revealable:
    _owner - Player which owns _wielder.  May be None.
    _wielder - the ControlledMonster or UncontrolledDenizen which wields this MonsterWeapon
    is_revealed() = _wielder.is_revealed()
    ...

CombatSheet:
    _owner - the Player which owns the _defender (if any).  This may be None.
    _defender - Character or Denizen which is defending on the CombatSheet
    placer_of(denizen) -> Player or None:
        # the Player who positions this Denizen on this CombatSheet
        if _owner != None:
            return _owner               # the owner of the CombatSheet positions everything on it
        return denizen._owner           # an unowned CombatSheet: the Denizen's own owner (None for an UncontrolledDenizen)
    ...

CharacterCombatSheet extends CombatSheet:
    _maneuver_chit_boxes - the horizontal section of Maneuver chit boxes at the bottom of the CharacterCombatSheet
    _shield_ovals - the ovals a shield can be placed in on the CharacterCombatSheet
    _used_this_round_box - the Used this Round box on the CharacterCombatSheet
    _charge_chits_box - the box on the CharacterCombatSheet showing the chits played to charge its _defender
    _attacker_boxes - the pink diagonal section boxes (1, 2, and 3) at the top left of the CharacterCombatSheet
    _attack_chit_boxes - the vertical section of Attack chit boxes at the top right of the CharacterCombatSheet
    ...

DenizenCombatSheet extends CombatSheet:
    _target_boxes - the horizontal row of boxes at the bottom of the DenizenCombatSheet
    _defender_boxes - the diagonal row of boxes in the upper left of the DenizenCombatSheet
    _other_attackers_boxes - the vertical column of boxes in the upper right of the DenizenCombatSheet
    ...

Player:
    _name - login name
    ...

CombatWindow:
    _owner - Player which owns the CombatWindow
    _battle_sheet_list - list of CombatSheet instances in the battle.  All CombatWindows contain the same set
                         of CombatSheet instances.  But the content shown on a CombatSheet can be different in
                         different CombatWindow instances.  Each players' CombatWindow will show the
                         CombatSheets of all participants in the battle, no matter who the owner of the
                         CombatSheet is (Character | Denizen).
    _step - the current combat step (e.g. Position, Tactics, Results)
    ...

Chit implements Revealable:
    # may be a Character chit or another entity behaving like a Character Chit.  e.g., glove/boots item
    _owner - Player which owns _character
    _character - Character which has the Chit
    is_revealed() -> bool
    ...

Weapon implements Revealable:
    _owner - Player which owns _character
    _character - Character which has the Weapon
    _placed_with - the Chit this Weapon is placed with: an AttackChit, or a ManeuverChit when used to parry.  May be None.
    is_revealed() = (_placed_with != None) and _placed_with.is_revealed()
    ...

Shield implements Revealable:
    _owner - Player which owns _character
    _character - Character which has the Shield
    is_revealed() -> bool
    ...

AttackChit extends Chit:
    _weapon - the Weapon used with this AttackChit, if any.  may be None.
    ...

ManeuverChit extends Chit:
    ...

CharacterHorse extends ManeuverChit:
    ...

AttackSpell extends AttackChit:
    ...

#####################################################
Rules for secrecy vs reveal, APPLIED IN STRICT ORDER:
#####################################################

 1. The state of secrecy of a Revealable object defaults to is_revealed() = True.
 2. Chits and Shields in a non-owner's CombatWindow return is_revealed() = False.
 3. Chits in a CharacterCombatSheet._used_this_round_box or ._charge_chits_box return is_revealed() = True.
 4. A Denizen shown on a CombatSheet returns is_revealed() = False in every CombatWindow whose owner is not
    CombatSheet.placer_of(Denizen), unless placer_of(Denizen) is None.

 5. A Revealable that defines is_revealed() in terms of another object (e.g., NativeHorse, MonsterWeapon, Weapon)
    follows the revealed state of said object.

 6. Revealable objects which have not been positioned yet by their owner are shown in their default positions.
 7. Revealable objects with is_revealed() = False are shown in their default positions.
 8. Revealable objects with is_revealed() = True are shown in their chosen positions.

 9. This must converge for a single Player BattleWindow to have is_revealed() = True for all Revealables.

10. From the Tactics step onward, every Revealable returns is_revealed() = True in every CombatWindow.
    This takes precedence over rules 1 - 5.

N.B., Character objects are not Revealable, but their counter is always shown in a CharacterCombatSheet they own.
      It is never shown on any other CombatSheet.
